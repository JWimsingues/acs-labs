== Lab objectives

By completing this lab, participants will:

. Navigate the RHACS vulnerability dashboard and understand how Scanner V4's Indexer and Matcher produce scan results for base OS and language-level dependencies.
. Create automated, scheduled vulnerability reports (PDF/CSV) scoped to specific namespaces.
. Build custom RBAC configurations with Permission Sets, Access Scopes, and Roles to enforce tenant isolation inside a shared ACS Central.
. Map OIDC (Keycloak) group claims to custom ACS roles and enable the OpenShift Console Plugin for developer self-service.
. Configure a generic webhook notifier to route policy violation alerts to external systems (OpenITCockpit / Nagios pattern).
. Integrate RHACS with Splunk via HEC (HTTP Event Collector) for SIEM correlation of policy breach events.
